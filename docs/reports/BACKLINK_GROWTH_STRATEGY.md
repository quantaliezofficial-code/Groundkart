## 3. Pillar 1: Venue Partner Co-Marketing & Embed Badges

Every venue onboarded to GroundKart has existing digital touchpoints (standalone websites, Google Business Profiles, Instagram bios, Facebook pages, and local community groups).

### 3.1 "Book Online on GroundKart" Partner Badge
Provide verified venue owners with an embeddable HTML badge for their website:

```html
<a href="https://groundkart.com/venues/grand-turf-arena" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
  <img src="https://groundkart.com/badges/verified-partner.svg" alt="Book Ground Online on GroundKart" width="180" height="48" />
</a>
```

### 3.2 Google Business Profile (GBP) Booking Button
Guide venue owners during onboarding to set their GBP primary "Book Online" appointment link directly to their GroundKart canonical URL (`https://groundkart.com/venues/:slug`). This drives hyper-relevant, authoritative referral traffic and strong local entity signals to Google.

## 4. Pillar 2: Local Sports Leagues & Tournament Partnerships

GroundKart operates in regions with vibrant amateur sports cultures (box cricket tournaments, corporate weekend football leagues, badminton championships).

### Execution Tactics:
1. **Tournament Booking Platform:** Act as the official venue booking and slot reservation partner for local weekend leagues.
2. **League Landing Pages:** Host dedicated tournament brackets and match location guides on GroundKart (`/tournaments/metropolis-corporate-cup-2026`), earning editorial links from corporate intranets, participant team blogs, and regional sports associations.
3. **University & College Sports Clubs:** Offer verified student club booking discounts in exchange for event coverage links on university sports portals.
